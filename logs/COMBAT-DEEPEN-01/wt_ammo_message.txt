MCT-COMBAT-DEEPEN-01: the ammunition half of the comparison is done, and FOUR of our five muzzle velocities are
EXACTLY the value the real game files state - with one named discrepancy.

docs/wt/wt-reference/WT_AMMO_COMPARISON.json reads the real gun files that the unit files name,
gamedata/weapons/groundmodels_weapons/125mm_2a46_2_user_cannon.blk and 120mm_rheinmetall_l44_user_cannon.blk, and
pairs each of our five shells with the round that matches, printing the pairing rule on every row.

THE RESULT: eng_125_apfsds_v1 1700 m/s equals 125mm_ussr_3BM42_APDS_FS 1700; eng_125_heat_v1 905 equals
125mm_ussr_3BK12_HEAT_FS 905; eng_120_apfsds_v1 1650 equals 120mm_NATO_APDS_FS 1650; eng_120_heat_v1 1140 equals
120mm_NATO_HEAT_FS 1140. Four exact matches out of five, and the exactness is what makes the pairing credible
rather than convenient.

THE ONE DISCREPANCY IS THE FINDING AND IS NAMED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED OVER: our engineering HE round declares
700 m/s while the game 125 mm HE round is 850 m/s, a 150 m/s shortfall. Nothing was changed this round; it is
recorded as an open item against the CD07 HE family.

TWO FURTHER MEASURED FACTS, shown side by side rather than declared equal because they are different quantities:
the T-80B gun file states shotFreq 0.16666 rounds per second, i.e. one shot every 6 s, against our project
reload_time of 7.1 s, and the Leopard gun file states 0.2, i.e. 5 s, against our 6 s. Our reloads are therefore
1.1 s and 1.0 s slower than the cadence the weapon file implies - a declared project choice that is now a
measured difference instead of an unknown.

ROUNDS THE GAME HAS THAT WE DO NOT MODEL, listed rather than ignored: the T-80B also fires 125mm_ussr_APDS_FS
(1760 m/s, 4.83 kg, small-core tungsten) and 125mm_ussr_ATGM (27.5 kg, atgm_tank - a guided missile), and the
Leopard 2A4 also fires 120mm_DM_APDS_FS (1640 m/s, 4.3 kg). Real round data is now available to argue the damage
model against: masses 4.85 / 19 / 23 kg, explosive types with masses (a_ix_2 3.402 kg on HE, a_ix_1 1.65 kg on
HEAT, comp_b 1.64 kg on the NATO HEAT round), bulletType, maxDistance, and the normalization and ricochet presets
each round names. One completeness item was also found on our side: our shells carry a source_bullet_type field
that does NOT name a real round, which is why the pairing needed the family keyword.

The boundary is unchanged and was checked again: the extraction and the tool live outside the repository, and only
derived values and provenance were committed.
