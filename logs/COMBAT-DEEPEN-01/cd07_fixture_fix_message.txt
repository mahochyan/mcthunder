MCT-COMBAT-DEEPEN-01 CD07 fixture repaired: the probe that reported "does not register" was wrong THREE times, all
of it mine, and the product truth it was hiding is now measured.

FAULT ONE, THE WRONG ENTRY POINT: L1 built the catalog as VehicleCatalog.new(packet.get("sources",{})), handing the
packet EVIDENCE sources where the catalog expects the project MODEL registry
(res://configs/vehicles/model_sources.json), so registration failed on the model binding - and the old code
asserted only "does not register" and printed no reason at all. It now uses the default constructor, which is the
same entry point production uses, and PRINTS the registration errors so a future failure explains itself.

FAULT TWO, NO PHYSICS BEFORE READING: L1 disabled physics on the actor and waited two PROCESS frames before
reading the shell options, while the rounds are installed by the loading step on PHYSICS frames. It therefore read
an empty list and reported a product gap that was really its own wiring. It now waits thirty frames with physics
running, exactly as run_engineering_runtime_checks does, and only then freezes the actor.

FAULT THREE, THE WRONG ID FORM: the runtime shell id is the packet-scoped id PREFIXED WITH THE VEHICLE ID, so the
round is ussr_t_80b_eng_125_he_v1 and not eng_125_he_v1. Comparing against the packet id made the leg report that
nothing offered the round while the printed option list immediately above it showed the round installed.

WHAT THE REPAIRED PROBE MEASURES, WHICH IS THE CD07 CLAIM ITSELF: the T-80B carries 125mm_2A46_2_user_cannon and
offers ussr_t_80b_shell, ussr_t_80b_eng_125_heat_v1 and ussr_t_80b_eng_125_he_v1; the Leopard carries
120mm_Rheinmetall_L44_user_cannon and offers only its default and HEAT round; the vehicles offering the engineering
HE are exactly ["ussr_t_80b"] - so the round is reachable on the vehicle that carries its gun AND limited to it,
1 of 2, with the Leopard explicitly checked not to offer it. RESULT: 13 checks, 0 failures,
CD07_HE_LANDED_PASS.

The L2 outcome is unchanged and still recorded honestly by the probe own note: terminal=expired_distance,
contacts=0, burst=none, because the fixture has nothing in the flight path - so that leg measures what happens when
the round meets nothing and does NOT claim a burst. The contact burst path itself is measured elsewhere and green:
probe_cd007_world_burst 15/0 and probe_cd007_occlusion_target 13/0, the latter recording terminal=internal_burst
with contact_kind=world_contact and three channels.

NO EXPECTATION WAS LOWERED. The failures that disappeared were failures of the fixture own wiring; the product
assertions are the same assertions, and the option list that proves the behaviour is printed as evidence. The HE
velocity alignment stays prepared rather than applied, and can now proceed as a declared behavior change with its
own migration entry.
