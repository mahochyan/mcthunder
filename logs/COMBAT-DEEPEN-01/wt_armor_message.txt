MCT-COMBAT-DEEPEN-01: the armour half of the comparison is done against the REAL game files, and the result is
that every armour figure this project declares is supported by the installed build.

docs/wt/wt-reference/WT_ARMOR_COMPARISON.json pairs each of our seventeen armor.* facts per vehicle with the
actual DamageParts of the local War Thunder 2.59.0.13 unit files, by a region rule that is printed in the record
next to every row rather than left implicit. The T-80B unit file carries 95 armour parts and the Leopard 2A4
carries 116.

THE OUTCOME, in full and without rounding up: 34 rows compared, 27 matched_exact (a real part carries exactly
that thickness), 7 candidates_include_it (the value is present among that region parts - supported, not unique),
ZERO matches_effective_max, and ZERO no_candidate. NOT ONE of our armour figures is unsupported by the installed
game files. Examples: T-80B hull front 80 = hull/body_front_dm, hull sides 80 = hull/body_side_dm, hull roof 30 =
hull/body_top_dm, hull rear 60 = hull/body_back_dm, hull floor 20 = hull/body_bottom_dm, turret front 250 =
turret/turret_front_dm, turret sides 157 = turret/turret_side_dm, turret roof 90 = turret/turret_03_top_dm, gun
shield 50 = turret/gun_mask_dm; Leopard hull front upper 400 = hull_front_composite_armor/body_front_dm, hull
front lower 35 = hull/superstructure_front_dm, turret front 250 = turret/turret_09_front_dm, turret sides 160 =
turret/turret_07_side_dm, gun shield 50 = mask/gun_mask_05_dm.

IT IS ALSO A VERSION CHECK: our packets record source_refs ["wt-2.57.1.137"] - the figures were copied from an
OLDER build dossier - while the install here is 2.59.0.13. All thirty four survive the version change, which is
recorded as exactly that claim and no more: the values are supported by the installed files, and the record names
the build they were checked against.

WHAT THE COMPARISON ADDS BESIDES CONFIRMATION: the real armour classes (RHA_tank_modern, CHA_tank_modern,
RHAHH_tank, titanium_alloy_vt6, tank_textolite, t_80b_composite_armor, tank_structural_steel), the real composite
layer structure (T-80B textolite layers at 50/50 mm and a 60 mm superstructure front with a 1.2 generic quality),
and per-part genericArmorQuality and armorEffectiveThicknessMax values that our material policy can now be argued
against instead of invented - while our protection.zone.* response profiles remain declared PROJECT policy, as
their own notes already state.

This is the first external comparison this project has ever had: every earlier row was NOT_COMPARED by
definition, and the armour rows now carry a named build, a real part and a real thickness. The boundary is
unchanged and was checked again: the extracted game data and the tool live outside the repository, and only
derived values and provenance were committed.
